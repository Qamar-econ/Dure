Introducing
Dure
Cooperating through messages.

Dure works just like a cooperative. A simple text from any phone is all it takes to help Noor get a fair price.

What stays

  • Pooling the harvest
  • Buyers bidding for the whole lot
  • One shared truck and pickup point
  • A record that earns trust

What goes

  • A legal entity
  • $1,000 in share capital
  • Fifteen founders who must agree
  • A board to run, a leader to fight over
Any phone. No internet. No app.SMS has been around since 1992. Dure works even on an old Nokia, and its central server pairs a small AI with a rules engine, so it runs cheaply and accurately.

9 · From the slope to the ministry

An automated Registry and AI policy suggestions.

A farmer Registry is important and effective, but labour-intensive and expensive to keep. Dure works as a market itself, and every deal leaves a footprint. So the Registry builds itself.

Policy makers and extension officers get accurate, good-quality information on limited resources.

AI is not a silver bullet. Noor knows farming better than Dure, so Dure stays out of how she farms. But when the extension officer visits Noor twice a year, they can make the best of it.

11 · Field problems

What are the real field problems?

The road washes out.

Dure books the truck and the pickup point. It can't fix the road, and doesn't pretend to.

Who sees the records?

Farmers' names and numbers stay with Dure. The ministry's Registry shows pseudonymous IDs and village totals, and only for farmers who said yes.

The power or the server goes down.

The rules engine keeps taking offers by text. The small model rechecks them later, and any change goes back to the farmer to confirm.

Electricity is a real problem in Timor-Leste.

40%of firms had power cuts in a year, losing 9.4% of sales.World Bank Enterprise Survey, 2015
24 houtages in Viqueque. Dili's hospital runs on generators each time.Tatoli, 2022–23
36%of Dili customers never report an outage.TANE survey, 2022

Dure always has a backup plan.

A rules engine reads every text instantly, with no model to load. Only the texts it isn't sure about go to the small model. If the server or the power fails, the rules engine keeps the pool open.
